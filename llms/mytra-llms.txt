# Mytra

> Mytra, Inc. is a Brisbane, California robotics company (founded 2022, led by former Tesla
> robotics lead Chris Walti) building "software-defined warehousing" — low-profile MytraBots
> that move in X, Y and Z through a modular steel cell matrix up to 80 feet tall, driven by an
> AI operating system that plans routes, resolves conflicts and reconfigures the matrix to
> store and retrieve loads up to 3,000 lb. The system is sold and installed as industrial
> infrastructure. As of 2026-08-04 Mytra publishes no developer program, no public API and no
> machine-readable API contract.
